Overall Assessment: Moderate Risk
This strategy contains 6 internal conflicts that could disrupt delivery. The most urgent is a compliance gap — the product is currently planned to launch before the legal review is complete. What This Means For You

Critical Issues · Action Required
Critical Issue 1 of 6
The product is scheduled to launch before the legal review is finished
The current plan requires the product to go live approximately 3 weeks before the compliance team has completed their checks. This means the business would be operating a live product that has not been legally cleared, creating a direct regulatory risk.
✅
Suggested Fix
Delay the product launch by 3 weeks, or restructure the plan so the legal review runs in parallel with the final development phase rather than after it.
High Issue 2 of 6
Two separate workstreams depend on the same single supplier — with no backup
Both the delivery team and the infrastructure team rely on the same third-party supplier at the same point in the schedule. If that supplier is delayed for any reason, it will stall both workstreams simultaneously rather than just one.
✅
Suggested Fix
Identify an alternative supplier that one of the two workstreams can use, or stagger the dependency so they do not both rely on the supplier at the same time.
High Issue 3 of 6
Budget approval is scheduled after procurement has already started
The plan requires procurement to begin before the budget has been formally approved. Without approval, procurement cannot proceed — meaning this sequence will create a hard stop the moment teams try to execute it.
✅
Suggested Fix
Move the budget approval gate to Week 2 of the programme, before any procurement activity begins.
