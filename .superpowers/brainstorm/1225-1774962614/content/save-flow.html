B — Two buttons: Save / Save & Restart

C — Hot-reload (no restart)

Save rewrites .env and calls a POST /api/v1/config/reload endpoint that calls load_dotenv(override=True) at runtime. No restart needed — env vars update in memory. Agents pick up new keys on their next call.
